How a project runs

Four elevations, one build

We borrow the term from architecture on purpose — each stage is a view of the same site, at a different level of detail.

EL. 01

Discover

We learn the business — who it serves, what it sells, and what a visitor needs to see in the first five seconds.

EL. 02

Design

Wireframes and visual direction, reviewed with you before a single line of code is written.

EL. 03

Build

Development, content placement and testing across devices — so the site works the same on a phone in traffic as it does on a laptop.

EL. 04

Launch

We go live, hand over what you need to update it, and stay reachable for the questions that come after.
